Add the creative tool's Stack editor shell. The Page sits on a desk, scaled down to fit narrower windows, with a Tools window that adds Elements, an Info window that edits the selection or the Page, and a strip of Pages to add, show and reorder them. Elements can be selected, Shift-selected, moved and resized. Names are checked as identifiers unique within the Page, and the Info window offers one when it refuses a name, or from a button's label (#749).
