Let a Folio Instrument kind's parameters name a `group` and a logarithmic `scale`, which the built-in kinds and Add-ons can declare. A synth's `attack`, `decay` and `release` now take a duration, such as `5 ms`, as well as a plain number read as seconds, and a Quantity of another Kind, such as `1 kg`, is `wrong kind`.
