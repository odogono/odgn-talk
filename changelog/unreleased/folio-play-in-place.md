Folio's Play button now plays every Project in place, keeping the windows, Palettes and Instruments window open, with Hush, Revert and the tempo and bar in the play controls. Project ▸ Present (⌘⇧↩) plays it full-window as a reader would see it. The separate Perform layout and the Browse tool are gone, and a Project's old `play` and a Palette's `float` settings are ignored (ADR 0174).
