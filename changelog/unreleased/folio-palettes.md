Folio Projects can have Palettes: panels of Elements authored like Pages and shown in their own floating windows during Browse and Present. A Palette's Scripts load when Play starts and keep running across Page changes until Stop; Scripts open and close it with `show` and `hide`, and the Window menu lists it while browsing. `.folio` format 11 saves Palettes in their own list (#1903).
