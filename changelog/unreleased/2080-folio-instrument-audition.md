Let Folio's Instruments window audition the selected Instrument in Edit. ▶ plays middle C for half a second, or a sampler's chosen sound, and plays again when a slider is let go or a value typed. ⌨ plays the Instrument from the computer keyboard, `A`–`K` and `W E T Y U` with `Z`/`X` to change octave, holding each note while its key is down (#2080).
