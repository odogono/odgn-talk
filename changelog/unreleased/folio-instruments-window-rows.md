Redesign Folio's Instruments window as compact rows in collapsible groups. Numbers get a slider, linear or logarithmic, beside a value you can type as a Quantity such as `5 ms`. A double-click or ⌥-click resets a slider and ⇧ makes it finer. A `+ ▾` menu adds and duplicates Instruments (⌘D), `−` or Delete removes one, and a copyable hint shows the Script lines for the selected Instrument.
