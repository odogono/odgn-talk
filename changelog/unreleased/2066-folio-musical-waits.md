Let Folio Scripts arrange a song with `wait 8 bars` and `wait 2 beats`, which end on the Transport's bar or beat line, so a `loop` straight after starts on that bar. Tempo and time-signature changes stretch the wait. Pause and a stopped Transport hold it, and Hush, Stop and Punch In end it. A time signature now takes effect at the next bar line, and the `transport` Grant's `beats` Operation is gone (ADR 0166).
