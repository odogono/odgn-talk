and 75.5 µs; instantiation 0.996, 0.971 and 0.972 ms. No p99 is above 10× its
p50. Raw compilation, which isn't gated, took 944 ms.

Memory is linear memory after `hello`, a Group, a small Script and one Pump. It
was 15.5 MiB (16,252,928 bytes) on all 20 instances.
