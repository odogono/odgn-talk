Each of these opens in [Folio](/folio/) as a fresh copy that you can play,
read and change. Nothing is shared back, and it keeps working offline.

## Sonar

[Open Sonar](/folio/sonar/)

A Minesweeper for the sea, drawn on one canvas. Click to ping, and right-click,
or hold a finger down, to plant a buoy. A radar sweep, the ripples and the
shockwave all come from one `frame` Handler, and `click` says where you pinged
and with which button.

## Tetris

[Open Tetris](/folio/tetris/) · [Open Canvas Tetris](/folio/canvas-tetris/)

The falling-blocks game, driven by the arrow keys and a `frame` loop. The first
repaints 200 rectangle Elements, and the second draws the same game with
canvas commands. Both share their rules through a Library and ship the same
Test Script.

## Lighthouse

[Open Lighthouse](/folio/lighthouse/)

A short illustrated mystery: Page navigation, showing and hiding Elements,
sounds, a `wait` and a journal the reader writes in.
